## Lessons
1. **Real client stories win.** The hospital testimonial gave chats at ₹14, against ₹170 for generic ads.
2. **Question hooks beat generic claims.** "Is your building fire-safe?" did better than "Protect your property with reliable…".
3. **There is demand for hydrant, sprinkler and fire-system work** (54 leads). These are high-value projects and deserve their own campaign with its own form questions.
4. **Refilling through Instant Form was expensive under IQRA (₹211/lead).** Refilling through WhatsApp now costs about ₹21 per chat. Keep refilling on WhatsApp.
5. **One broad ad set across the whole state, plus 39 ads, spread the money too thin.** Keep few ads per ad set and target the serviceable area only.
