## 4. Video brief for the in-house editing team

### Rules for every video
- **Phone number:** only 77720 78610. Never show the van or any old number (78601, 78605, 78970 13490).
- **No claims like:** "NOC guarantee", "100% safe", "cheapest". Fixed timelines only after the team confirms them.
- **Faces:** our staff are OK. Client staff only with consent; otherwise blur. Blur phone numbers and ID cards.
- **Language:**
  - On-screen text in simple English (our clients are professionals).
  - Voice-over can be Hindi + English mix for Reels, but captions stay in English.
- **Format:**
  - 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) for page, Reels and Shorts.
  - Plus 16:9 (1920×1080) for YouTube and the website hero.
  - Burned-in captions: assume the video plays without sound.
- **Branding:**
  - Logo in a corner. Colours: navy #004890, orange #E87010.
  - End card: "THE FIRE WALA · Hydrant · Sprinkler · Fire Alarm · Book a FREE site survey · 77720 78610".
- **Pacing:** 2–4 seconds per shot. Show real site footage only, no stock footage.

### Video A — "Project proof" (30–40 s) ⭐ main page video
**Goal:** show that we do the full job end-to-end. Use one real project, e.g. Hotel Harshpushp Garden.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Wide shot of the building (hotel/hospital/factory) | "Hotel project · Complete fire protection" |
| 3–8 s | Survey: engineer measuring, drawing on tablet/paper | "1. Site survey & design" |
| 8–14 s | Pipes being welded, painted red, fixed on supports | "2. Our own team installs the pipeline" |
| 14–19 s | Pump room: main, jockey and diesel pumps, panel | "3. Pump room set up" |
| 19–25 s | Hydrant flow test: water jet, pressure gauge close-up | "4. Tested at full pressure" |
| 25–30 s | Handover: file given to client, staff training | "5. Documents & training at handover" |
| 30–35 s | End card | "Book a FREE site survey · 77720 78610" |

### Video B — Founder talk "Why fire projects fail at handover" (45–60 s)
**Goal:** expert trust and the NOC worry. Founder speaks to camera, in office or on site.

**Script (English; a Hindi version is OK for Reels):**
> "If you are building a hospital, hotel, school or factory, your fire system is checked at the very end — at handover and at the Fire NOC inspection.
> That is where most problems show up: a quote that missed half the scope, pipes that don't match the drawing, a pump that was never tested, or no documents for the NOC file.
> At The Fire Wala, our own team of 50+ people does the survey, design, installation and testing — and gives you the drawings, test reports and handover documents for your NOC file.
> The NOC is issued by the fire department, but we make sure our part is right the first time.
> Planning a project? Book a free site survey. Call 77720 78610."

B-roll to cut in: site photos, the pump room, the flow test, a file being handed over.

### Video C — "Compare before you sign" (20–30 s, Reel)
**Goal:** filter out pure price shoppers and attract quality buyers.
- Text on screen, one line every 2 seconds, over site footage:
  "Comparing fire quotes? Check: 1 Pipe class & make · 2 Pump set specs · 3 Pump room scope · 4 Civil & electrical scope · 5 Testing included? · 6 Handover documents? · 7 Who attends after handover?"
- End: "Lowest quote ≠ lowest cost. Get a written BOQ after a FREE survey. 77720 78610"

### Video D — Client testimonial (20–40 s)
**Goal:** "people like me trust them".
- Interview a hotel GM, hospital admin or factory owner for 20–40 seconds.
- Questions to ask:
  1. "What did The Fire Wala do at your site?"
  2. "How was the team on site?"
  3. "How was the handover and documents?"
- Needs: written consent from the client, and their name and designation on screen.

### Video E — Testing proof (10–15 s loop, no talking)
Shots in this order: water jet, pressure gauge needle rising, pump starting.
- Used as a muted background loop in the page's hero section.

**Priority:** first A, then B. These two will improve the page the most. Then D, C and E.
