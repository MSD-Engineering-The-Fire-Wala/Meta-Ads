## Part A: Ready WhatsApp replies (save them as Quick Replies)

**How to save:** WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Quick replies → Add.
- Each reply gets a shortcut such as `/hi`.
- When Anu types `/hi` in a chat, the full message appears.
- Fill in the words in [brackets] before sending.

Language: send the English version by default. If the customer writes in Hindi, use the Hindi version.

### /hi: first reply (send within 5 minutes during working hours)
**English**
> Hello! Thank you for contacting The Fire Wala (MSD Engineering), Raipur. 🙏
> To share your quote quickly, please tell us:
> 1️⃣ How many extinguishers need refilling?
> 2️⃣ Type: ABC powder / CO2 / Foam / Water (a photo of the cylinder label also works)
> 3️⃣ Site location (area / city)
> 4️⃣ Company or organisation name
>
> Refilling from ₹100/kg + GST · 24–48 hours (depends on type) · Certificate + next-due-date sticker on every cylinder.

**Hindi**
> Namaste! The Fire Wala (MSD Engineering), Raipur se sampark karne ke liye dhanyavaad. 🙏
> Aapka quote jaldi bhejne ke liye kripya batayein:
> 1️⃣ Kitne cylinder refill karne hain?
> 2️⃣ Type: ABC powder / CO2 / Foam / Water (cylinder ke label ki photo bhi bhej sakte hain)
> 3️⃣ Site ki location (area / shehar)
> 4️⃣ Company / sanstha ka naam
>
> Refilling ₹100/kg + GST se · 24–48 ghante (type ke hisaab se) · Har cylinder par certificate + next-due-date sticker.

### /photo: if they don't know the type
> No problem. Please send a clear photo of the cylinder and its label. Our team will identify the type and size and share the rate.

### /quote: after the 4 answers (fill in the numbers)
> Thank you, [Name] ji. Your quote:
> • [Qty] × [Type] [Size] kg → ₹[amount] + GST
> • Turnaround: 24–48 hours (depends on type)
> • Pickup & drop: [free for 4+ cylinders (conditions apply) / please share address for pickup charges]
> • Refilling certificate + next-due-date sticker included
> Shall we schedule the pickup? Please share a convenient date and time.

### /followup: no reply after 24 hours
> Hello [Name] ji, just following up on your fire extinguisher refilling quote. Would you like us to schedule the pickup? If you have any questions, call us at 77720 78610.

### /lost: no reply after 3 follow-ups (then mark the lead Lost / Not interested in the tracker)
> Hello [Name] ji, we have not heard back, so we are closing this request for now. Whenever your extinguishers are due, just message us here. Stay safe! 🙏

### /afterhours: outside Mon–Sat 10 am – 7 pm
> Thank you for your message! Our office hours are Mon–Sat, 10 am – 7 pm. Please share the cylinder quantity, type and location, and our team will reply first thing during working hours.

**Never write in chat:**
- "100% safe", "NOC guarantee", "sabse sasta" or "cheapest"
- spare-part charges, trolley/50 L units, pickup km limits; say "conditions apply – our team will confirm"
- any Foam/Water discount
- any number other than 77720 78610

## Part B: Which ad brought which person (tracking)

### B1. Today (no change to ads)
WhatsApp Business shows a small **ad preview at the top of a chat** that started from a Meta ad. Anu fills the tracker from that:

| Tracker column | What Anu writes |
|---|---|
| Source | Meta WhatsApp / Meta Form / Google / Website / Referral / Walk-in |
| Campaign / Ad | The video seen at the top of the chat: **"Kajal video"** or **"Godown video"**, plus the person's segment (hospital / school / hotel / factory / office / shop / society) |

Limit: the Kajal video runs in 2 ads (Hospital + School/Hotel), and the Godown video runs in 2 ads (Factory + Office/Shop). The segment the person tells us is used to separate them.

### B2. From 1 Dec (Year-End ads): exact ad codes
Each new ad gets a **different pre-filled WhatsApp message with a short code**, for example:
- Hospital ad: "Hi, I want a refilling quote. [YE-H]"
- School/Hotel ad: "… [YE-S]"
- Factory ad: "… [YE-F]"
- Office/Shop ad: "… [YE-O]"

Anu copies the code into the "Campaign / Ad" column. This gives 100% exact ad → person → order tracking.
(Live ads cannot be edited for this without restarting learning, so the codes start with the new Year-End ads.)

### B3. Tracker stages Anu must keep updated
New → Contacted → Qualified (4 answers received) → Quote sent → Won / Lost, plus the Quote ₹ and Won value ₹.
**Claude reads this every Monday:** cost per Qualified lead, cost per Quote, and cost per Won order, for each ad.

## Part C: Buying behaviour → better ads (the loop)

1. **Collect (Anu, daily):**
   - segment, quantity, type, area, decision maker (owner / safety officer / admin / purchase)
   - time to reply, stage, won value, lost reason
2. **Analyse (Claude, weekly):** which segment, quantity band, area, role, day and time turns into Won orders, and at what ad cost.
3. **Decide (Dilshad, weekly):**
   - move budget to the ads and segments that bring Won orders
   - change messages, adding what winners care about (speed, certificate, pickup, price) and removing what doesn't work
4. **Show it again (Meta audiences, no customer list upload):**
   - *Retarget:* people who messaged us on WhatsApp/Page and people who watched 50%+ of our videos in the last 30–60 days. They see a reminder or proof ad (certificate, pickup photo, review).
   - *Find similar people:* a Lookalike audience built from people who messaged us, kept to the serviceable area.
5. **Behind the scenes vs front:** winners' questions show what to put in ads; lost reasons show what to fix in operations (price clarity, pickup speed, follow-up).

**⚠️ Needs a decision before use:** uploading phone numbers of customers (Won list) to Meta for audiences needs a **DPDP consent decision**. Until then, only Meta's own engagement audiences (people who messaged or watched) are used.
